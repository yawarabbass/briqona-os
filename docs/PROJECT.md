# BRIQONA OS

Version: 1.0
Status: Planning
Project Type: Multi-Tenant AI Business Operating System (SaaS)

---

# 1. Introduction

BRIQONA OS is a cloud-based, AI-powered Business Operating System (SaaS) that helps businesses manage their complete operations from a single platform.

The platform is designed to support multiple industries such as Office, Retail, POS, Restaurant, Hospital, School, Manufacturing, Construction, Real Estate, Professional Services, and more.

Businesses subscribe to a plan, receive their own secure workspace, and manage their operations using industry-specific modules.

# 5. Business Model

BRIQONA OS is a subscription-based SaaS platform.

A visitor comes to the public website.

The visitor can:

• Learn about the platform

• Explore features

• Explore industries

• Compare pricing

• Request a demo

• Purchase a subscription plan

After a successful subscription:

• Company Workspace is created

• Company Owner account is created

• Subscription is activated

• Login credentials are provided

The customer can then access the SaaS platform.

# 7. Master Panel

The Master Panel is controlled only by the BRIQONA OS platform owner.

The Master Panel manages:

• Companies

• Subscription Plans

• Industries

• Modules

• Features

• Billing

• Users

• Permissions

• AI Configuration

• Platform Settings

• System Monitoring

Everything inside the SaaS platform is controlled from the Master Panel.

---

# 8. Company Workspace

Each customer receives an isolated company workspace.

Inside the workspace, the Company Owner manages:

• Dashboard

• Employees

• Departments

• Roles

• Permissions

• Business Modules

• Reports

• Company Settings

Each company's data is completely isolated from every other company.
