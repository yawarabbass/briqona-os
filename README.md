# BRIQONA OS

### Business Operating System for Modern Businesses

BRIQONA OS is a modern business operating platform designed to bring
business operations, intelligence, analytics and automation together
in one unified workspace.

The project is being developed with a focus on simplicity, scalability,
modern user experience and responsible technology.


## Overview

BRIQONA OS aims to provide businesses with a unified operating layer
for managing important areas of their day-to-day operations.

The platform concept includes:

- Business dashboard
- Customer management
- Order management
- Product management
- Inventory
- Analytics and reporting
- AI-powered business intelligence
- Workflow automation
- Business insights
- Workspace management
- Security and privacy considerations

Product Direction

The long-term direction of BRIQONA OS includes connecting the frontend
experience with a secure backend, authentication, database services,
AI integrations, automation, analytics and subscription billing.

These capabilities will be implemented progressively as the product
develops.

AI

BRIQONA AI is intended to serve as an intelligence layer for business
operations.

Potential capabilities include:

Business questions and answers
Operational summaries
Trend detection
Business insights
Workflow assistance
Decision support
Intelligent automation

Live AI functionality requires a secure backend/API integration.
Security

Security and responsible data handling are important considerations
in the development of BRIQONA OS.

The production platform may require additional security controls,
authentication, authorization, secure backend infrastructure,
monitoring and other protections as development progresses.

Privacy

BRIQONA OS is designed with privacy and responsible handling of
business information in mind.

Please review the project's Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions
before using production services.

Pricing

The website may display example subscription plans for the product
experience.

Actual billing, subscription management and payment processing require
a production billing backend and payment provider integration.

Development Status

Status: Active Development

The current project is a frontend/product experience and is being
developed toward a complete business operating platform.

Features may change as development continues.

Deployment

The project can be deployed using static hosting platforms such as
GitHub Pages or Vercel for the frontend experience.

Production backend services will be connected separately when ready.
