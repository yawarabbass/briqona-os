Cookie overview

What cookies do on a website.

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites can store in a browser. They can help websites remember settings, understand usage and provide useful functionality.

Important

Cookie use depends on the feature.

BRIQONA OS may use cookies or similar technologies where they are needed to provide website functionality, remember preferences, improve the experience or understand how services are used.

01

Essential cookies

These can support core website functions, security, navigation and other features necessary for the service to work properly.

02

Preference cookies

These can help remember choices and settings so the experience can be more consistent when you return to the website.

03

Analytics cookies

Where enabled, analytics technologies can help understand website usage and identify areas where the experience can be improved.

Why similar technologies may be used

  • Remembering user preferences and settings.
  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Helping protect the website and its users.
  • Understanding how pages and features are used.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Supporting features that require stored information.
