Questions

Pricing without the confusion.

Everything you need to know before choosing your BRIQONA OS workspace.

Can I change my plan later? +

Yes. You can start with the $5 Starter plan and move to Growth or Scale as your business requirements increase.

Is there a free trial? +

A 3-day demo experience can be offered before choosing a paid workspace plan. The payment system can be connected when the backend is ready.

Does every plan include a dashboard? +

Yes. The Starter plan includes the core business dashboard, while higher plans add advanced intelligence, reporting, automation and controls.

Can I cancel anytime? +

The frontend is prepared for subscription management. Cancellation and billing controls can be connected to the backend payment system later.

Which plan should I choose? +

Starter is ideal for a small operation. Growth is designed for growing businesses that need analytics and automation. Scale is for teams requiring deeper controls and advanced operations.
