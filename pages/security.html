Security is an ongoing responsibility.

BRIQONA OS is being developed with security, privacy and responsible data handling as important considerations. Specific technical controls and security capabilities may evolve as the platform and its infrastructure develop.

Data & access

A secure business workspace depends on appropriate access controls and careful handling of operational information.

  • Workspace access should be limited to authorized users.
  • Business information should be handled responsibly.
  • Account permissions can be expanded as platform capabilities develop.
  • Security controls should evolve alongside the product.

AI & privacy

BRIQONA AI is designed as an intelligence layer for business operations. AI capabilities should operate with appropriate privacy and security considerations.

  • Avoid exposing unnecessary sensitive information.
  • AI outputs should be reviewed when business decisions require human judgment.
  • Backend AI integrations should use appropriate authentication and access controls.
  • AI security practices can evolve as integrations and infrastructure mature.
